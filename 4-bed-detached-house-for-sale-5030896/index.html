About the property

Description property

DNG Brian MacMahon is delighted to bring to the market this fantastic 4-bedroom detached home with detached garage. The house is situated on a 0.54 acre site in the quiet countryside just 7.1km from Gort Town. The property has been freshly painted throughout and would be ideal for first time buyers and for families looking to upsize.

Immediately to your right off the hallway is the sitting room with dual aspect windows, patio door and feature fireplace with solid fuel stove. The good-sized kitchen boasts white fitted wall and floor units giving an abundance of storage space. Off the kitchen is the utility room which provides further storage space. At the end of the hallway is the light filled large living room with vaulted ceiling and feature fireplace with a solid fuel stove. Completing the ground floor is a beautifully presented shower room and a spacious double bedroom.

Upstairs off the landing are three generously proportioned double bedrooms with the master boasting an ensuite and walk-in wardrobe. The family bathroom completes this fantastic rural home.

Gort is a busy, friendly market town with excellent childcare facilities and both Primary and Secondary schools available, a choice of supermarkets, restaurants, pharmacies, and hotel with full leisure centre and lots of sporting amenities. Gort has excellent transport links with easy access to the M18 & M6 motorways linking Galway to Limerick and Dublin.
